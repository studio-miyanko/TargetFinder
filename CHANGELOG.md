# Target Finder

## Unreleased

### Targeting

- FIND targets the way RestedXP's targeting macro does. Names match exactly and the top row wins. A corpse makes FIND step back to your previous target.
- FIND puts a skull on the enemy it targets, through the game's own `/tm` command. In a group only the party leader, raid leader or a raid assistant gets the skull.
- Target Finder no longer sets raid markers by itself, because WoW Forever restricts that for addons. The eight slot markers are gone.
- FIND is now a character macro and is never created twice at login.

### Quest NPCs

- A Quest NPCs box fills free rows with the closest kill, loot and turn-in NPCs of your quests and keeps them current as quests and zones change. It replaces the Add Quest NPCs button.
- Quest rows show Questie's kill, loot or turn-in icon.
- Droppers of a container that holds a quest item count too.
- Names you add stay until you remove them, also quest NPCs picked from the suggestions.
- A quest suggestion puts that quest's NPCs first.

### Window and menus

- Drag FIND from the window's icon to an action bar. The macro book no longer opens by itself.
- The help line names the next step: place FIND, then press it.
- The first NPC suggestion is highlighted, so Enter takes its full name.
- Track in a unit menu puts the NPC first. Chat stays quiet for changes made in the open window.
- The window remembers its position and `/tf minimap` hides or shows the minimap button. The tooltip says when Questie failed to start.

### Removed

- Track First, Clear All, Add All and the minimap right-click.
- The Assist macro, Shift-click to clear on the minimap button, Clear in unit menus and Target Finder entries on friendly players.
